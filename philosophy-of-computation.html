Philosophy of Computation

Founded at UC Berkeley

Philosophy of Computation at Berkeley was a student organization based at UC Berkeley, mostly active during 2017-2018. With the tagline "computer science as an end in itself", we offered weekly sessions and a DeCal course called The Poetry of Computer Science, the Computer Science of Poetry.

Miara wrote a textbook and taught the course in Fall 2017 and Spring 2018. Our goal was to offer a more rigorous, ethical, and humanistic alternative to the scourge of effective altruism and rationalism that was entrapping computer science students on campus. We partially succeeded, but almost ten years have passed, and Miara is looking to reboot the project to redeem the Bay Area. If you'd like to help with this project, get in touch at miarasung at gmail dot com.
